# Comparison of the human and the machine translations

Phase 2 of [`language-support-plan.md`](../docs/language-support-plan.md). The 13 catalogues that people wrote
(`translations/human/`) were compared, message by message, with versions generated by a language model
(`translations/machine/`); the result is in `assets/locale/`.

**Rule.** The human entry is kept. A missing, fuzzy (never shown) or placeholder-breaking human entry is filled from the machine
version. A human entry is replaced only where it is plainly wrong (listed below, with the reason). The machine version cannot
judge which wording a native speaker prefers, so entries that differ in wording are kept as the human wrote them, and the
few where the meaning looks doubtful are listed for a speaker to review.

`translator-credits` and the translated product name are the human's, kept as written: the credit is the only record of
who translated, and it is shown as such on the About page even though gaps were filled by a model.

## Human entries replaced

- `ar` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "مفاتيح التعديل يجب أن تكون دائمًا بين علامتي تنصيص" replaced by "يجب وضع مفاتيح التعديل بين قوسين زاويين" ("between quotation marks" (علامتي تنصيص) instead of angle brackets).
- `ar` "No skin tone": "إخفاء ألوان البشرة" replaced by "بدون لون بشرة" ("hide skin tones": an action, not the absence of a tone).
- `cs` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "Modifikační klíče musí být ve špičatých závorkách" replaced by "Klávesy modifikátorů musí být v lomených závorkách" ("klíče" (cryptographic keys) for keyboard keys).
- `cs` "List of valid key names": "Seznam platných jmen klíčů" replaced by "Seznam platných názvů kláves" ("jmen klíčů" (names of cryptographic keys) for key names).
- `cs` "Medium skin tone": "Jemně tmavá barva pleti" replaced by "Střední barva pleti" ("Jemně tmavá" means slightly dark, not medium (text adapted to the catalogue's own "barva pleti")).
- `de` "List of valid key names": "Liste der gültigen Schlüsselnamen" replaced by "Liste gültiger Tastennamen" ("Schlüsselnamen" (names of cryptographic keys) for key names).
- `es` "translator-credits": "créditos-traductor" replaced by "translator-credits" (the words translated literally ("créditos-traductor"), not a name: it showed as the translator's name).
- `es` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "Las teclas modificadoras deben estar entre los cheurones" replaced by "Las teclas modificadoras deben ir entre paréntesis angulares" ("cheurones" is not a Spanish word).
- `es` "List of valid key names": "Lista de nombres clave válidos" replaced by "Lista de nombres de tecla válidos" ("nombres clave" (key as in password) for key names).
- `gl` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "As teclas modificadores deben estar entre cheuróns" replaced by "As teclas modificadoras deben ir entre parénteses angulares" ("cheuróns" is not a Galician word).
- `gl` "List of valid key names": "Lista de nomes de chaves válidas" replaced by "Lista de nomes de tecla válidos" ("chaves" (key as in password) for keyboard keys).
- `it` "List of valid key names": "Lista dei nomi chiave validi" replaced by "Elenco dei nomi di tasto validi" ("nomi chiave" (key as in password) for key names).
- `nl` "List of valid key names": "Lijst met geldige waardenamen" replaced by "Lijst met geldige toetsnamen" ("waardenamen" means value names, not key names).
- `nl` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "Sneltoetsen dienen tussen chevrons te worden geplaatst" replaced by "Wijzigingstoetsen moeten tussen punthaken staan" ("Sneltoetsen" means shortcuts, not modifier keys).
- `oc` "Contributors": "Contributors" replaced by "Contribuidors" (the English word left as it is).
- `oc` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "Las claus de modificacions devon èsser entre croquets" replaced by "Las tòcas modificatrises devon èsser entre parentèsis angularas" ("croquets" are square brackets, not angle brackets).
- `pl` "Use a keyboard shortcut to toggle the menu": "Użyj skrót klawiszowy by pokazać menu" replaced by "Używaj skrótu klawiszowego do przełączania menu" (ungrammatical ("Użyj skrót", should be "skrótu")).

## Human entries kept that a speaker should review

- `de` "Modifier keys must be between chevrons": "Änderungstasten müssen zwischen den Chevrons sein" ("Chevrons" is an English loan word where "spitze Klammern" would be usual).
- `es` "If you access the menu with the keyboard shortcut, you don't need to always display the icon.": "Si accedes al menú con el atajo de teclado, puede que no necesites que se muestre siempre el icono." ("puede que no necesites" hedges what the source states).
- `nl` "Medium light skin tone": "Lichtere huidskleur" ("Lichtere" (lighter) next to "Lichte" (light) loses the grade).
- `nl` "Medium dark skin tone": "Donkerder huidskleur" ("Donkerder" (darker) next to "Donkere" (dark) loses the grade).
- `nl` "Don't use a keyboard shortcut that already exists.": "Deze sneltoets is al in gebruik." (says "this shortcut is already in use", which is a statement, not the instruction).
- `oc` "Use a keyboard shortcut to toggle the menu": "Utilizar un acorchi de clavièr per dobrir lo menú." (says "to open" the menu, not to toggle it).
- `zh_CN` "translator-credits": "校对" ("校对" means proofreader: a role, not a name).
- `zh_CN` "Light skin tone": "微淡色" (the skin-tone words (微淡色, 淡色, 中色, 偏暗色, 暗色) leave out "skin").
- `zh_CN` "Type here to search…": "搜索…" ("搜索…" drops "type here").
